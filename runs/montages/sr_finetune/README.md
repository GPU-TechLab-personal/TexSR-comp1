## Before reading

- Map montages and map metrics show one checkpoint per fine-tuned arm (step 0); render and perceptual
  metrics cover every step in the table above.
- The 1-channel maps (roughness, metallic, height) went in as three equal channels and are the mean of the
  three output channels; how far those channels drifted apart is `channel_spread` in each `sr_manifest.json`.
- The `render` column of `<material>.png` is image SR of MatUp's LR render; `render_<material>.png` and
  `overview_render_pred.png` render each method's predicted SVBRDF with MatUp's renderer.
